Own the system — or rent it forever?

SaaS tools (invoicing apps, lightweight CRMs, productivity suites) win when you need team seats, automatic payment collection, or client portals. Most solo freelancers and small operators do not need that yet — they need visibility and fewer logins.

Where competitors are strong

Etsy ADHD planners: huge review volume, GoodNotes formats, low price entry, visual variety.

Notion / Softr CRMs: relational databases, free tiers, modern UI.

SaaS invoice tools (~$9/mo): send invoices by email, multi-currency, polish.

Where they are weak (our opening)

  • Marketplace noise — hard to know which template is maintained.
  • Monthly fees for problems that are mostly tracking, not automation.
  • 250-page planners that increase decision load for ADHD brains.
  • No clear “start here” path — catalogs without problem framing.
