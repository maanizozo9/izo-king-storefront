Field notes

Three things we keep seeing

Short observations — not a course. Useful framing for people who sell services and run small operations.

01 · Offers

Demand was assumed, not tested

Building for weeks then silence is expensive. A seven-day test — one small signal a day — tells you keep, narrow, or pause.

02 · Money

Invoices hide in chats and memory

When clients and overdue amounts live in five places, follow-up slips. One Excel system with overdue flags is often enough.

ADHD days need less clutter

Printable planners work when they respect energy — structure light enough to open on a hard morning.
