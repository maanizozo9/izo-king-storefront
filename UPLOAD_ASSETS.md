## How (GitHub website)
1. Open https://github.com/maanizozo9/izo-king-storefront
2. Click Add file → Upload files
3. Drag the `assets` and `media` folders
4. Commit to main
5. Wait ~1 minute for Vercel
6. Open https://izo-king-studio.vercel.app/
