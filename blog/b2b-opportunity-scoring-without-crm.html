B2B opportunity scoring without another CRM

Most agency pipelines fail for a simple reason: every new lead is treated as equal. A company that already buys similar services sits next to a brochure site with no contact path. Your team spends the same time on both.

Opportunity scoring is a practical way to rank accounts before outreach. It does not replace judgment. It forces a shared language: Fit (does this look like a buyer for your service?), Need (are there public signs of demand or gaps?), Timing (is the site active, hiring, launching?), Reachability (is there a real way to contact them?), and Evidence (how much of that is on the page versus assumed?).

You do not need a full CRM to start. You need:

  • A public company URL
  • A short score with transparent dimensions
  • Labels that separate Known facts from Inferred guesses and Unknown gaps
  • One next action and a place to track it

Heavy CRMs become useful when many people share stages, forecasts, and reporting. Solo operators and small agencies often only need prioritization and a clean follow-up list.

This week’s action: pick ten target companies. For each, open the public homepage, score Fit/Need/Timing in under five minutes, and write one outreach draft only for the top three. Skip the rest until next week.
