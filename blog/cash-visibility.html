Cash visibility beats complex accounting software

Many small businesses open an accounting tool and never maintain it. What they actually need is a weekly picture: what came in, what went out, and whether next month is safe.

A focused finance tracker or cash-flow framework can surface problems early — late invoices, silent expense creep, or a product that looks busy but does not pay.

Start with one dashboard you open every Friday. Expand only when that habit is real.
