Why ADHD brains need systems, not more apps

If you live with ADHD, downloading another planner app rarely fixes the day. Reports and practitioner guides in 2026 still point to the same friction: time blindness, task initiation, and too many open loops.

What works better is a visible system with fewer choices: a daily/weekly board you can see, a morning routine that does not depend on motivation, and exam tracking that does not rely on memory alone.

IZO tools such as The Clear Start planner, ADHD Morning Routine Bundle, and The Study Start exam tracker are built as simple digital systems — not another social feed of tasks.

Try this week: pick one planning surface only. Move tomorrow’s top three tasks there tonight. Ignore the rest of the tool stack until that habit sticks.
